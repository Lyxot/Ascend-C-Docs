  <h1 class="topictitle1">基于自定义算子工程的算子开发</h1>
  <div id="body0000001702292369">
   <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p15234921195016">本节以一个简单算子为例，带您体验从算子工程创建、代码编写、编译部署到运行验证的开发全流程，让您对算子开发工程有个宏观的认识，此处我们以输入是动态shape的Add算子实现为例，为了与内置Add算子区分，定义算子类型为AddCustom。</p>
   <div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__section5367539134913">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">工程创建</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p185837654610">CANN软件包中提供了工程创建工具<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0017.html">msOpGen</a>，开发者可以输入算子原型定义文件生成Ascend C算子开发工程。</p>
    <ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001502825998_ol1626512810440">
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001502825998_li1426528194416"><span>编写AddCustom算子的原型定义json文件。json文件的配置参数详细说明请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0021.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370267097__zh-cn_topic_0000001740005677_table8534182020116">表1</a>。</span>
      <p></p>
      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001502825998_p948313013819">假设AddCustom算子的原型定义文件命名为add_custom.json，存储路径为: $HOME/sample，文件内容如下：</p>

      <p></p></li>
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001502825998_li89761353472"><span>使用msOpGen工具生成AddCustom算子的开发工程。</span>
      <p></p>
      <pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__screen62467580520"><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__b84441629201020"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph1163892310460">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/python/site-packages/bin/msopgen gen -i $HOME/sample/add_custom.json -c ai_core-<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph53071420178"><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001682787961_i79331727136">&lt;soc_version&gt;</em></span> -lan cpp -out </strong><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__b555752141112">  $HOME/sample/AddCustom</strong></pre>
      <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ul814311210385">
       <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li1735619823819"><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__b9823114104919"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph1282312410496">${INSTALL_DIR}</span></strong>为CANN软件安装后文件存储路径，请根据实际环境进行替换。</li>
       <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li1127452442312">-i：算子原型定义文件add_custom.json所在路径。</li>
       <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li595191863811">-c：ai_core-<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001502825998_ph81480341636"><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002400092065_i79331727136">&lt;soc_version&gt;</em></span>代表算子在AI Core上执行，<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001502825998_ph14215154001316"><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002400092065_i79331727136_1">&lt;soc_version&gt;</em></span>为<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph1555712386204">昇腾AI处理器</span>的型号。

          <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ul1124912113117">
           <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_li048917175169">非<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_ph126871512141617"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_zh-cn_topic_0000002336176798_term1253731311225">

             </term></span>：在安装<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_ph196874123168">昇腾AI处理器</span>的服务器执行<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b17687612191618">npu-smi info</strong>命令进行查询，获取<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b10161437131915">Name</strong>信息。实际配置值为AscendName，例如<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b16284944181920">Name</strong>取值为<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_i1478775919179">xxxyy</em>，实际配置值为Ascend<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_i1678775901719">xxxyy</em>。</li>

              Atlas A3 训练系列产品/Atlas A3 推理系列产品
             </term></span>：在安装<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_ph17911124171120">昇腾AI处理器</span>的服务器执行<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_zh-cn_topic_0000001264656721_zh-cn_topic_0000001117597244_b206066255591">npu-smi info -t board -i </strong><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_zh-cn_topic_0000001264656721_zh-cn_topic_0000001117597244_i16609202515915">id</em><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_zh-cn_topic_0000001264656721_zh-cn_topic_0000001117597244_b14358631175910"> -c </strong><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_zh-cn_topic_0000001264656721_zh-cn_topic_0000001117597244_i16269732165915">chip_id</em>命令进行查询，获取<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b11257114917192">Chip Name</strong>和<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b72671651121916">NPU Name</strong>信息，实际配置值为Chip Name_NPU Name。例如<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b13136111611203">Chip Name</strong>取值为Ascend<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_i68701996189">xxx</em>，<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_b51347352112">NPU Name</strong>取值为1234，实际配置值为Ascend<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_i82901912141813">xxx</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002336350326_i154501458102213">_</em>1234。

      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001491362920_p144703124147">命令执行成功后，自定义算子包中的相关文件将部署至当前环境的OPP算子库的vendors/customize目录中，如果用户部署多个自定义算子包，可通过如下命令指定路径安装：</p>
      <pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__screen17267134515506">./custom_opp_<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i19269174545011">&lt;target os&gt;</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i72692457505">_</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i82691945145020">&lt;target architecture&gt;</em>.run --install-path<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i1126934518501">=&lt;path&gt;</em></pre>
      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p5841248135020">说明：如果部署算子包时通过配置--install-path参数指定了算子包的安装目录，则在使用自定义算子前，需要执行source <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i113273265120">&lt;</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i632153235110">path</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i3327328513">&gt;</em>/vendors/<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i932932175115">&lt;vendor_name&gt;/</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i759482541811">bin/set_env.bash</em>命令，set_env.bash脚本中将自定义算子包的安装路径追加到环境变量ASCEND_CUSTOM_OPP_PATH中，使自定义算子在当前环境中生效。</p>

    <h4 class="sectiontitle">算子ST测试</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p7896195217520">CANN开发套件包中提供了ST测试工具“msOpST”，用于生成算子的ST测试用例并在硬件环境中执行。</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p198731559122716">本节仅以AddCustom算子为例，介绍ST测试工具的关键执行流程。</p>
    <ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ol1486618151343">
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li586614152419"><span>创建算子ST测试用例定义文件“AddCustom_case.json”，例如存储到跟算子工程目录“AddCustom”同级别的“AddCustom_st”路径下。</span>
      <p></p>
      <div class="p" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p1081213052612">
       “AddCustom_case.json”文件的样例如下，开发者可基于此文件定制修改。
       <pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__screen145461847867">[

        "case_name": "Test_AddCustom_001", 
        "op": "AddCustom", 
        "input_desc": [ 
            {
                "format": [
                    "ND"
                ],
                "type": [
                    "float16"
                ],
                "shape": [8,2048],
                "data_distribute": [ 
                    "uniform"
                ],
                "value_range": [ 
                    [
                        0.1,
                        1.0
                    ]
                ],
                "name": "x"
            },
            {
                "format": [
                    "ND"
                ],
                "type": [
                    "float16"
                ],
                "shape": [8,2048],
                "data_distribute": [
                    "uniform"

      </div>
      <p></p></li>
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li3233551161012"><span>配置ST测试用例执行时依赖的环境变量。</span>
      <p></p>
      <div class="p" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p13344162224820">
       如下为设置环境变量的示例。<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph270424465219">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>表示CANN软件安装目录，<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph1311473410913">例如，$HOME/Ascend/ascend-toolkit/latest</span>。<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i679195575819">{arch-os}</em>为运行环境的架构和操作系统，<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i1619111018593">arch</em>表示操作系统架构，<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i1170424412528">os</em>表示操作系统，例如x86_64-linux或aarch64-linux。
       <pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000001541959061_screen16433645173910">export DDK_PATH=<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph4287101844820">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>
export NPU_HOST_LIB=<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph42877184487">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i1428741819483">{</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__i8287101815485">arch-os}</em>/devlib</pre>
      </div>
      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__p8897182451320">提示：请根据CANN软件包实际安装路径对以上环境变量进行修改。</p>
      <p></p></li>
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li1122812571411"><span>进入msOpST工具所在目录，执行如下命令生成并执行测试用例。</span>
      <p></p>
      <ol type="a" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ol1892141710239">
       <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li169211717162312">进入msOpST工具所在目录。
        <pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__screen2989123515140">cd $HOME/Ascend/ascend-toolkit/latest/python/site-packages/bin</pre></li>
       <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li12737182622310">生成测试用例文件并执行。
        <pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__screen18968144611236">./msopst run -i $HOME/AddCustom_st/AddCustom_case.json -soc <span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph21258112114"><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__zh-cn_topic_0000002400092065_i79331727136_3">&lt;soc_version&gt;</em></span> -out $HOME/AddCustom_st</pre>
        <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ul2679141011246">
         <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li1967971018249">-i：算子测试用例定义文件（*.json）的路径，可配置为绝对路径或者相对路径。</li>
         <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li1998015471226">-soc：<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__ph19994533233">昇腾AI处理器</span>的型号，请根据实际环境进行替换。</li>
         <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336176826__li33571354122318">-out：生成文件所在路径。</li>
