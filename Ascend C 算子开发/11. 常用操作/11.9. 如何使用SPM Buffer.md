  <h1 class="topictitle1">如何使用SPM Buffer</h1>
  <div id="body8662426">
   <div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__zh-cn_topic_0000001611158057_section147991226121118">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能介绍</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__p1761857101618">SPM Buffer功能支持对<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph978064310219">Unified Buffer</span>的暂存操作，当<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph1876611474619">Unified Buffer</span>内存有溢出风险时，可以将<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph07751918114616">Unified Buffer</span>的数据先拷贝到SPM(Spill Memory) Buffer暂存，具体使用时再取回。</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__p251381319714">使用方法如下：</p>
    <ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ol18210134010719">
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__li1421018401673">调用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0166.html">InitSpmBuffer</a>接口完成初始化操作。</li>
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__li2050016476713">调用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0167.html">WriteSpmBuffer</a>接口完成数据的拷贝暂存操作。</li>
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__li133906121499">需要使用暂存的数据时，调用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0168.html">ReadSpmBuffer</a>接口完成数据的取回操作。</li>
    </ol>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__p20837525104712">针对<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph14504143371016"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__zh-cn_topic_0000002336176798_term11962195213215">
       Atlas A2 训练系列产品/Atlas 800I A2 推理产品/A200I A2 Box 异构组件
      </term></span>，仅支持暂存到workspace。</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__p91531273575">针对<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph9153152715719"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__zh-cn_topic_0000002336176798_term1253731311225">
       Atlas A3 训练系列产品/Atlas A3 推理系列产品
      </term></span>，仅支持暂存到workspace。</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__p187861018114718">针对<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph194756336178"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__zh-cn_topic_0000002336176798_term71949488213">
       Atlas 训练系列产品
      </term></span>、<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph19951183013136"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__zh-cn_topic_0000002336176798_term4363218112215">
       Atlas 推理系列产品
      </term></span>，可以暂存到workspace或者<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336698__ph540018302214">L1 Buffer</span>。</p>
   </div>
