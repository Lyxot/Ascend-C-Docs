<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__section417961104715"><h4 class="sectiontitle">问题根因</h4><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__p19149174017319">当前操作用户缺少对部署路径下vendors目录的写权限。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__p314116416265">自定义算子包默认安装路径<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__ph14149155162212">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/opp/vendors的目录权限与CANN软件包安装用户和安装配置有关：root用户安装CANN，<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__ph153184126411">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/opp/vendors权限为755；非root用户携带--install for all参数安装CANN，该目录权限为755，非root用户不带--install for all参数安装CANN时，该目录权限为750。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__p789254122311">例如，root用户安装CANN软件包后，HwHiAiUser属组用户在对应目录部署自定义算子包，因为其他用户没有写权限，会出现上述报错信息，提示权限不足导致自定义算子包部署失败。</p>
</div>
<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__section12149153520416"><h4 class="sectiontitle">处理步骤</h4><ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__ul185211144306"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__li95211514113012">方法一：使用<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__i7851113322616">--install-path</em>参数并配置环境变量ASCEND_CUSTOM_OPP_PATH来指定安装目录（参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/opdevg/Ascendcopdevg/atlas_ascendc_10_0069.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370175005__li1652971821912">指定目录安装</a>）。运行用户需要对指定的安装路径有可读写权限。<pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__screen18733373512">./custom_opp_&lt;target os&gt;_&lt;target architecture&gt;.run --install-path=&lt;path&gt;
source <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__i167713265431">&lt;</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__i117719269431">path</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__i12771226124318">&gt;</em>/vendors/<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__i2077192674319">&lt;vendor_name&gt;/</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__i759482541811">bin/set_env.bash</em></pre>
</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__li1197610202348">方法二：联系CANN软件包安装用户修改默认安装路径下的vendors目录权限，比如修改为777：<pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336610__screen493964731218">chmod 777 /usr/local/Ascend/latest/opp/vendors/</pre>
</li></ul>
