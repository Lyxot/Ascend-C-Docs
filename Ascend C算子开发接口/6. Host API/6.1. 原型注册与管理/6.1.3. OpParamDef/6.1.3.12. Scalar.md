<h1 class="topictitle1">Scalar</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>配置该参数后，自动生成的单算子API（aclnnxxx）接口中，输入类型为aclScalar类型。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><div class="code_wrap" codetype="Cpp"><div class="highlight"><table class="highlighttable"><tr><td class="linenos"><div class="linenodiv"><pre><span class="normal">1</span></pre></div></td><td class="code"><div><pre><span class="n">OpParamDef</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&amp;</span><span class="n">Scalar</span><span class="p">()</span>
</pre></div></td></tr></table></div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">参数说明</h4><p>无</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">返回值说明</h4><p>OpParamDef算子定义，OpParamDef请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0957.html">OpParamDef</a>。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">约束说明</h4><ul><li>仅支持对算子输入做该参数配置，如果对算子输出配置该参数，则配置无效。</li><li>该接口仅在如下场景支持：<ul><li>通过单算子API执行的方式开发单算子调用应用。</li><li>间接调用单算子API(aclnnxxx)接口：Pytorch框架单算子直调的场景。</li></ul>
</li></ul>
</div>
