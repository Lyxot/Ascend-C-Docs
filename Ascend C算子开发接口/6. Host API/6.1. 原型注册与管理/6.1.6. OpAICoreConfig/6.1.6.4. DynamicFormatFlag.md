<h1 class="topictitle1">DynamicFormatFlag</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>标识是否根据<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0982.html">SetOpSelectFormat</a>设置的函数自动推导算子输入输出支持的dtype和format。设置为<span class="parmvalue">“true”</span>，则无需在原型注册时配置固定的dtype与format，会调用推导函数来推导算子输入输出支持的dtype和format。</p>
</div>
