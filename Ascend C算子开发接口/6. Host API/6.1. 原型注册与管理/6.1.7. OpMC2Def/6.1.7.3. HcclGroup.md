<h1 class="topictitle1">HcclGroup</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>配置通信域名称，每个名称对应一个通信域。配置后在kernel侧调用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_1219.html">GetHcclContext</a>接口可获取通信域对应的context（消息区）地址。</p>
</div>

<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">约束说明</h4><p>使用该接口前，算子需要先通过<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0954.html">MC2</a>接口注册该算子是通算融合算子，注册后即返回一个<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0999.html">OpMC2Def</a>结构。</p>
<p>通信域名称必须先配置为REQUIRED String类型的属性，属性名即为通信域名称。</p>
