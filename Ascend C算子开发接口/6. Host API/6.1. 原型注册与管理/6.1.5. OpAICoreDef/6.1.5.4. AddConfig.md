# AddConfig

<div>
<div class="section">
<h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
<p>注册算子支持的AI处理器型号以及<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0988.html">OpAICoreConfig</a>信息。</p>
</div>
<div class="section">
<h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4>
<pre><code class="language-cpp">void AddConfig(const char *soc)
void AddConfig(const char *soc, OpAICoreConfig &amp;aicore_config)
</code></pre>
</div>
