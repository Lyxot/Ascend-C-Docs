  <h1 class="topictitle1">SetViewShape</h1>
  <div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p>设置aclTensor的ViewShape。ViewShape表示aclTensor的逻辑shape，可由<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_1072.html">GetViewShape</a>获取。</p>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4>
    <p><strong>void SetViewShape(const op::Shape &amp;shape)</strong></p>
   </div>
