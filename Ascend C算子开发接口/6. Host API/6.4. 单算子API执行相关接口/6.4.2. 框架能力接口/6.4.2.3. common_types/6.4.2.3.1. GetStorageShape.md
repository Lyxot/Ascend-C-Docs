  <h1 class="topictitle1">GetStorageShape</h1>
  <div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p>获取aclTensor的StorageShape属性。</p>
    <p>OriginShape表示aclTensor在经历transdata节点前（如果存在该节点）的原始shape信息，即tensor的shape数学描述。</p>
    <p>StorageShape表示aclTensor在内存上的实际排布，即OriginShape实际运行时的shape格式。</p>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4>
    <p><strong>gert::Shape GetStorageShape()</strong></p>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">参数说明</h4>
    <p>无</p>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">返回值说明</h4>
    <p>返回值为<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/basicdataapi/atlasopapi_07_00149.html">gert::Shape</a>，记录了一组shape信息，例如一个三维shape：[10, 20, 30]。</p>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">约束说明</h4>
    <p>无</p>
   </div>
