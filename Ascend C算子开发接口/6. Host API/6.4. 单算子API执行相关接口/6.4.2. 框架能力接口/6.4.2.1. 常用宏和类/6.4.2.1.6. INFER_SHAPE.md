<h1 class="topictitle1">INFER_SHAPE</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">宏功能</h4><p>针对指定算子，运行其InferShape函数，推导输出shape。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">宏原型</h4><p><strong>INFER_SHAPE(KERNEL_NAME, op_args...)</strong></p>
<p>如下接口是上述宏定义会调用到的关联接口。</p>
<p>OP_INPUT(x...)</p>
<p>OP_OUTPUT(x...)</p>
<p>OP_ATTR(x...)</p>
<p>OP_WORKSPACE(x...)</p>
<p>OP_OUTSHAPE(x...)</p>
<p>OP_OPTION(x...)</p>
<p>OP_EMPTY_ARG</p>
<p>OP_MODE(x...)</p>
</div>
