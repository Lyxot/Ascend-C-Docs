<h1 class="topictitle1">L2_DFX_PHASE_1</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">宏功能</h4><p>用于L2一阶段接口aclnn<em>Xxx</em>GetWorkspaceSize时延统计及入参打印，必须在一阶段接口最前方调用。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">宏原型</h4><p><strong>L2_DFX_PHASE_1（APIName, IN, OUT）</strong></p>
<p>如下接口是上述宏定义会调用到的关联接口。</p>
<p>#define DFX_IN(...) std::make_tuple(__VA_ARGS__)</p>
<p>#define DFX_OUT(...) std::make_tuple(__VA_ARGS__)</p>
</div>
