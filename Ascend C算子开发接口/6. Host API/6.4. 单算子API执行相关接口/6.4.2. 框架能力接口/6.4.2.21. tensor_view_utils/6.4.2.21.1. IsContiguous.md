<h1 class="topictitle1">IsContiguous</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>判断输入tensor是否为一个连续tensor。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><p><strong>bool IsContiguous(const aclTensor *tensor)</strong></p>
</div>

<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">返回值说明</h4><p>若为连续tensor返回true，否则返回false。</p>
