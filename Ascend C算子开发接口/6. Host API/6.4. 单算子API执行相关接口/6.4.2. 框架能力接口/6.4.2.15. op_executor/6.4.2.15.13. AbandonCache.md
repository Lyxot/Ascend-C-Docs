<h1 class="topictitle1">AbandonCache</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>关闭aclnn cache缓存功能。</p>
<p>该功能是指首次调用aclnn算子后框架会记录cache缓存，后续再次调用时，将跳过一阶段aclnnXxxGetWorkspaceSize函数，从而达到性能优化的目的。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><p><strong>void AbandonCache(bool disableRepeat = false)</strong></p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">参数说明</h4>

<th align="left" class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="15.409999999999998%"><p>输入/输出</p>
</th>
<th align="left" class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="67.36999999999999%"><p>说明</p>

</td>
</tr>

</div>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">返回值说明</h4><p>无</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">约束说明</h4><p>对于一阶段aclnnXxxGetWorkspaceSize函数必须要执行的场景，需要在aclnnXxxGetWorkspaceSize函数中显示调用AbandonCache。</p>
</div>
