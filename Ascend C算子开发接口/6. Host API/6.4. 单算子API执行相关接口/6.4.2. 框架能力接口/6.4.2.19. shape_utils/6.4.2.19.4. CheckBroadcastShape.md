<h1 class="topictitle1">CheckBroadcastShape</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>校验两个shape间是否满足broadcast关系（广播规则介绍参考<a href="https://numpy.org/doc/stable/user/basics.broadcasting.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">NumPy</a>官网）。例如[2, 1]与[2, 10]满足broadcast关系，[2, 2]与[2, 10]不满足broadcast关系。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><p><strong>bool CheckBroadcastShape(const Shape &amp;self, const Shape &amp;other)</strong></p>
</div>
