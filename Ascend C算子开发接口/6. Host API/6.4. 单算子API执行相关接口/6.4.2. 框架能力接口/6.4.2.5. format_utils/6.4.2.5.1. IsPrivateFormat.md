  <h1 class="topictitle1">IsPrivateFormat</h1>
  <div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p>判断输入的format是否为私有格式。</p>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4>
