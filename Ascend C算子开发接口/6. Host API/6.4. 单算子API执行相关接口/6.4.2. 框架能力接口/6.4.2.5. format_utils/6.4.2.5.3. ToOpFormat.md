  <h1 class="topictitle1">ToOpFormat</h1>
  <div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p>将aclFormat转为对应的op::Format。</p>
    <ul>
     <li>aclFormat：AscendCL（Ascend Computing Language）框架对外提供的数据格式，具体定义参见<span>“<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/appdevgapi/aclcppdevg_03_1348.html">aclFormat</a>”</span>。</li>
     <li>op::Format：算子框架内部定义的数据格式，本质使用的是GE定义的<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/basicdataapi/atlasopapi_07_00515.html">ge::Format</a>。</li>
    </ul>
   </div>
   <div class="section">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4>
    <p><strong>Format ToOpFormat(aclFormat format)</strong></p>
