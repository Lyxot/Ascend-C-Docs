<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">约束说明</h4><ul><li>调用本接口前确保已调用过<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0871.html">Init</a>和<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_10047.html">SetCcTiling</a>接口。</li><li>入参handleId只能使用Prepare原语对应接口的返回值。</li><li>非<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_10162.html">细粒度通信</a>时，本接口的调用次数应该与Prepare的repeat次数一致。<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_10162.html">细粒度通信</a>时，本接口的调用次数应该与通信任务的总步骤数/步长*Prepare的repeat次数一致。</li><li>本接口在AI Cube核或者AI Vector核上调用必须与对应的Prepare接口的调用核保持一致。</li></ul>
