<h1 class="topictitle1">使用说明</h1>
<div><p><span>Ascend C</span>提供一组Conv3DBackpropFilter高阶API，便于用户快速实现卷积的反向运算，求解反向传播的误差。</p>
<p>卷积反向的权重传播如<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__fig1710410547569">图1</a>，卷积反向权重计算如<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__fig25291917533">图2</a>。</p>
<p>Conv3dBackpropFilter的计算公式为：</p>
<p><img class="mathml" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_formulaimage_0000002336335434.png"></p>
<ul><li>X为卷积的特征矩阵Input。</li><li>∂L/∂Y为卷积正向损失函数对输出Y的梯度GradOutput，作为求反向传播误差∂L/∂W的输入，即卷积的输出反向GradOutput。</li><li>∂L/∂W为Weight权重的反向传播误差GradWeight。</li></ul>
<div class="fignone"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__fig1710410547569"></a><a name="fig1710410547569"></a><span class="figcap"><b>图1 </b>卷积反向权重传播示意图</span><br><img class="eddx" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_image_0000002336175658.png"></div>
<div class="fignone"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__fig25291917533"></a><a name="fig25291917533"></a><span class="figcap"><b>图2 </b>卷积反向权重计算过程示意图</span><br><img class="eddx" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_image_0000002336335438.png"></div>
