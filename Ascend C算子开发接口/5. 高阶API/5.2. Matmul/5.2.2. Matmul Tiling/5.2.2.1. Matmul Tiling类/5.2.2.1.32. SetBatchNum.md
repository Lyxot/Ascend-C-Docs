<h1 class="topictitle1">SetBatchNum</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>设置多Batch计算的最大Batch数，最大Batch数为A矩阵<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0648.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333538__table9646134355611">batchA</a>和B矩阵<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0648.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333538__table9646134355611">batchB</a>中的最大值。调用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0642.html">IterateBatch</a>接口之前，需要在Host侧Tiling实现中通过本接口设置多Batch计算的Batch数。</p>
</div>
