  <h1 class="topictitle1">MatmulConfig</h1>
  <div>
   <p>模板参数MatmulConfig，用于配置Matmul模板信息以及相关的配置参数。不配置默认使能Norm模板，Norm模板的介绍请参考<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336332840__table6981133810309">表 模板特性</a>。MatmulConfig的参数说明见<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336332840__table1761013213153">表2</a>。MatmulConfig的定义方式有：</p>
   <ul>
    <li>该模板参数可选取提供的模板之一，当前提供的MatmulConfig模板取值范围为【CFG_NORM、CFG_MDL、CFG_IBSHARE_NORM、MM_CFG_BB】，分别对应默认的Norm、MDL、IBShare、BasicBlock模板。各模板的介绍请参考<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336332840__table6981133810309">表1</a>。</li>
    <li><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336332840__li0460173613513"></a><a name="li0460173613513"></a>该模板参数可以基于各类获取模板的接口，自定义模板参数配置，获取自定义模板。各类获取模板的接口包括：<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0621.html">GetNormalConfig</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0622.html">GetMDLConfig</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0623.html">GetSpecialMDLConfig</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0624.html">GetIBShareNormConfig</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0625.html">GetBasicConfig</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0626.html">GetSpecialBasicConfig</a>。</li>
    <li>另外，MatmulConfig可拆分为<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0627.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table16317184295116">MatmulShapeParams</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0627.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table8313111211573">MatmulQuantParams</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0627.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table15129204644">MatmulBatchParams</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0627.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table66217141862">MatmulFuncParams</a>二级子Config，使用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0627.html">GetMMConfig</a>接口，设置需要的二级子Config和<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0627.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table17837129144319">MatmulConfigMode</a>，可以更加灵活的获取自定义的模板参数配置MatmulConfig。</li>
   </ul>
