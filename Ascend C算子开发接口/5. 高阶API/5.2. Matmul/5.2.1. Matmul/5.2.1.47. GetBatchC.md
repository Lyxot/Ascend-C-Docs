<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p><a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0637.html">GetBatchTensorC</a>接口与该接口的功能相同，建议使用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0637.html">GetBatchTensorC</a>。</p>
<p>调用一次GetBatchC，会获取C矩阵片，该接口可以与<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0644.html">IterateNBatch</a>异步接口配合使用。用于在调用IterateNBatch迭代计算后，获取一片std::max(batchA, batchB) * singleCoreM * singleCoreN大小的矩阵分片。</p>
</div>
