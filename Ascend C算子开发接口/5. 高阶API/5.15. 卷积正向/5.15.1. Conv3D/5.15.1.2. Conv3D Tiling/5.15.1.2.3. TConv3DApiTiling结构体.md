<h1 class="topictitle1">TConv3DApiTiling结构体</h1>
<div><p>TConv3DApiTiling结构体包含Conv3D算子规格信息及Tiling切分算法的相关参数，被传递给Conv3D Kernel侧，用于数据切分、数据搬运和计算等。TConv3DApiTiling结构体的参数说明见<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370171485__table18244199192620">表1</a>。</p>
<p>用户通过调用<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_10083.html">GetTiling</a>接口获取TConv3DApiTiling结构体，具体流程请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_10080.html">使用说明</a>。当前暂不支持用户自定义配置TConv3DApiTiling结构体中的参数。</p>
