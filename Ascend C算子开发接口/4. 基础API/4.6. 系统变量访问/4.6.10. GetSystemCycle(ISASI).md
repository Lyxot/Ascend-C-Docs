<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>获取当前系统cycle数，若换算成时间需要按照50MHz的频率，时间单位为us，换算公式为：time = (cycle数/50) us 。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><div class="code_wrap" codetype="Cpp"><div class="highlight"><table class="highlighttable"><tr><td class="linenos"><div class="linenodiv"><pre><span class="normal">1</span></pre></div></td><td class="code"><div><pre><span class="n">__aicore__</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kr">inline</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">int64_t</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">GetSystemCycle</span><span class="p">()</span>
</pre></div></td></tr></table></div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">参数说明</h4><p>无</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">返回值</h4><p>返回int64_t类型的系统cycle数。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">约束说明</h4><p>该接口是PIPE_S流水，若需要测试其他流水的指令时间，需要在调用该接口前通过<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0271.html">PipeBarrier</a>插入对应流水的同步，具体请参考<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252085__li126441923175612">调用示例</a>。</p>
</div>
