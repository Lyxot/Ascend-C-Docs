  <h1 class="topictitle1">LoadTilingLibrary</h1>
  <div id="body8662426">
   <div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__zh-cn_topic_0000002078486173_zh-cn_topic_0000001576727153_zh-cn_topic_0000001389787297_section36583473819">
    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__p1276464131817">根据输入的路径，加载对应的Tiling动态库。开发者基于<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/opdevg/Ascendcopdevg/atlas_ascendc_10_0058.html">工程化算子开发</a>开发方式完成算子实现后，可通过<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__b193041939135010">算子包编译</strong>或<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__b6588174665019">算子动态库编译</strong>获取对应的Tiling动态库文件。</p>
    <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__ul20736843111817">
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__li12737204311184">算子包编译：Tiling实现对应的动态库为算子包部署目录下的liboptiling.so。具体路径可参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/opdevg/Ascendcopdevg/atlas_ascendc_10_0069.html">算子包部署</a>。</li>
    </ul>
    <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__ul8812125117535">
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333080__li1727495710535">动态库编译：Tiling实现集成在算子动态库libcust_opapi.so中。具体路径可参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/opdevg/Ascendcopdevg/atlas_ascendc_10_00012.html">算子动态库编译</a>。</li>
    </ul>
