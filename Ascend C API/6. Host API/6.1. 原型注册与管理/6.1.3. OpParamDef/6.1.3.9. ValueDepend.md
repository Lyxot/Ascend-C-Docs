    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370172645__zh-cn_topic_0000001626329929_p8469112415249">标识该输入是否为“数据依赖输入”，数据依赖输入是指在Tiling/InferShape等函数实现时依赖该输入的具体数据。该输入数据为host侧数据，开发者在Tiling函数/InferShape函数中可以通过TilingContext类的<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/basicdataapi/atlasopapi_07_00225.html">GetInputTensor</a>/InferShapeContext类的<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/basicdataapi/atlasopapi_07_00096.html">GetInputTensor</a>获取这个输入数据。</p>
