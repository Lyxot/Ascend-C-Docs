    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__p117354985217">灵活的自定义Matmul模板参数配置。通过设置<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table17837129144319">MatmulConfigMode</a>、<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table16317184295116">MatmulShapeParams</a>、<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table8313111211573">MatmulQuantParams</a>、<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table15129204644">MatmulBatchParams</a>、<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__table66217141862">MatmulFuncParams</a>，获取自定义的<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0616.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336332840__table1761013213153">MatmulConfig</a>。</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173446__p119591129337">MatmulConfigMode指定了获取并要修改的MatmulConfig模板，各模板介绍请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0616.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336332840__table6981133810309">模板特性</a>；用户根据使用需求通过设置可变参数，即一个或多个任意顺序的MatmulShapeParams、MatmulQuantParams、MatmulBatchParams、MatmulFuncParams，修改该MatmulConfig模板的相应参数配置。相比<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0621.html">GetNormalConfig</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0622.html">GetMDLConfig</a>等获取模板的接口，该接口提供了更灵活的自定义Matmul模板参数的配置方式。</p>
   </div>
