  <h1 class="topictitle1">使用说明</h1>
  <div id="body0000002100033038">
   <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__p196475214312">Ascend C提供一组Conv3D高阶API，方便用户快速实现3维卷积正向矩阵运算。3维正向卷积的示意图如<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig198021635102613">图1</a>，其计算公式为：</p>
   <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__p1444414795217"><span><img class="mathml" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__image7684735310" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_formulaimage_0000002336334694.png"></span></p>
   <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__ul1132562017378">
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li163251920163718">X为Conv3D卷积的特征矩阵Input。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li153252020183716">W为Conv3D卷积的权重矩阵Weight。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li332582003711">B为Conv3D卷积的偏置矩阵Bias。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li63251120143713">Y为完成卷积及偏置操作之后的结果矩阵Output。</li>
   </ul>
   <div class="fignone" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig198021635102613">
    <a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig198021635102613"></a><a name="fig198021635102613"></a><span class="figcap"><b>图1 </b>3维正向卷积示意图</span>
    <br><span><img class="imgResize" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__image141491119165120" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_image_0000002370252901.png" title="点击放大"></span>
    <div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__note5724151414442">
     <img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span>
     <div class="notebody">
      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__p198991658115617">Cin为Input的输入通道大小Channel；Din为Input的Depth维度大小；Hin为Input的Height维度大小；Win为Input的Width维度大小；Cout为Weight、Output的输出通道大小；Dout为Output的Depth维度的大小；Hout为Output的Height维度大小；Wout为Output的Width维度大小；下文中提及的M维度，为卷积正向操作过程中的输入Input在img2col展开后的纵轴，数值上等于Hout * Wout。</p>
      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__p049418510351">Channel、Depth、Height、Width后续简称为C、D、H、W。</p>
     </div>
    </div>
   </div>
   <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__p116281841183416">除上述基础运算外，在Conv3D计算中可以设置参数Padding、Stride和Dilation，具体含义如下。</p>
   <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__ul17885132073612">
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li1888612063615">Padding代表在输入矩阵的三个维度上填充0，见<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig16852164019363">图2</a>。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li163327475402">Stride代表卷积核三个维度上滑动的距离，见<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig137769564018">图3</a>。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li2761443154215">Dilation代表卷积核三个维度上每个数据的间距，见<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig1015315044111">图4</a>。</li>
   </ul>
   <div class="fignone" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__fig16852164019363">

   <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__p106721512348">使用Conv3D高阶API实现Conv3D运算的具体步骤如下：</p>

    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li14445242457">创建Conv3D对象。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li204441424164513">初始化操作。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li1744412243450">设置3D卷积输入Input、Weight、Bias和输出Output。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li544415248452">完成3D卷积操作。</li>
    <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336333144__li1944410244451">结束3D卷积操作。</li>
