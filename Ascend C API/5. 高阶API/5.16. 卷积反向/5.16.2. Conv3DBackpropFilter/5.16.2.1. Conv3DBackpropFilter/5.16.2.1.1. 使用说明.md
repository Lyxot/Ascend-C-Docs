<h1 class="topictitle1">使用说明</h1>
<div id="body0000002067300278"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__p49401268286"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__ph975583562710">Ascend C</span>提供一组Conv3DBackpropFilter高阶API，便于用户快速实现卷积的反向运算，求解反向传播的误差。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__p1194013263283">卷积反向的权重传播如<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__fig1710410547569">图1</a>，卷积反向权重计算如<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__fig25291917533">图2</a>。</p>

<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__p18495581146"><span><img class="mathml" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336173982__image16724115731615" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_formulaimage_0000002336335434.png"></span></p>
