    <h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__p424021522518">依次计算按元素求和、结果进行deq量化后再进行relu计算（结果和0对比取较大值）。计算公式如下：</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__p6677202911263"><span><img class="mathml" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__image14254101915112" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_formulaimage_0000002370253837.png"></span></p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__p880611302345">Deq的计算公式如下：</p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__p2827204703418"><span><img class="mathml" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__image95421456113513" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_formulaimage_0000002336175846.png"></span></p>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336174190__p12691658113512">如上公式先除以2^17再乘以2^17用于防止x乘以DeqScale出现溢出情况；公式中DeqScale需要通过SetDeqScale进行设置，具体可参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/atlasascendc_api_07_0099.html">SetDeqScale</a>。</p>
