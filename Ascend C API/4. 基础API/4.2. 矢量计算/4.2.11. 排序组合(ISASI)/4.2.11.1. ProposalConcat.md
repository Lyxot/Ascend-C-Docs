<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__section618mcpsimp"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p10462291478">将连续元素合入Region Proposal内对应位置，每次迭代会将16个连续元素合入到16个Region Proposals的对应位置里。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p192611347476"><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__b626118471875">Region Proposal说明：</strong></p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p63615432233">目前仅支持两种数据类型：half、float。</p>
<div class="p" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p28787592117">每个Region Proposal占用连续8个half/float类型的元素，约定其格式：<pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__screen164011814162216">[x1, y1, x2, y2, score, label, reserved_0, reserved_1]</pre>
</div>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p1739222113177">对于数据类型half，每一个Region Proposal占16Bytes，Byte[15:12]是无效数据，Byte[11:0]包含6个half类型的元素，其中Byte[11:10]定义为label，Byte[9:8]定义为score，Byte[7:6]定义为y2，Byte[5:4]定义为x2，Byte[3:2]定义为y1，Byte[1:0]定义为x1。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p13442715126">如下图所示，总共包含16个Region Proposals。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p4380141412118"><span><img id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__image188831148119" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_image_0000002336175786.png"></span></p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p9273112710318">对于数据类型float，每一个Region Proposal占32Bytes，Byte[31:24]是无效数据，Byte[23:0]包含6个float类型的元素，其中Byte[23:20]定义为label，Byte[19:16]定义为score，Byte[15:12]定义为y2，Byte[11:8]定义为x2，Byte[7:4]定义为y1，Byte[3:0]定义为x1。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p135820202115">如下图所示，总共包含16个Region Proposals。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__p2092535916137"><span><img id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370252193__image898718317149" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/API/ascendcopapi/figure/zh-cn_image_0000002370173917.png"></span></p>
