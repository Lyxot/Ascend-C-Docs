<h1 class="topictitle1">生成测试用例定义文件</h1>
<div id="body0000002329913125"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__p8060118">指导用户使用msOpST工具生成算子测试用例定义文件（*.json），作为算子ST测试用例的输入。</p>
<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__section07359458247"><h4 class="sectiontitle">使用方法</h4><ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__ol347991341719"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li03205217308"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li03205217308"></a><a name="li03205217308"></a><span>获取待测试算子的信息定义文件（.ini）路径。</span><p><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__p1510016517394">msOpST工具根据待测算子信息库定义文件（.ini）生成算子ST测试用例定义文件，在算子工程文件中算子信息库定义文件路径如下所示。</p>
<pre class="screen" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__zh-cn_topic_0229823833_screen81802763412">├── <strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__b1183912541496">cpukernel</strong>                             //AI CPU算子文件目录
│   ├── CMakeLists.txt
│   ├── impl        //算子代码实现文件目录
│   │   ├── <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i16568838632">xx</em>_kernels.cc
│   │   └── <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i19320141632">xx</em>_kernels.h
│   ├── op_info_cfg
│   │   ├── <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i17521744732">xx</em>_kernel
│   │       <strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__b74699255012">├── <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i32104481133">xx</em>.ini</strong><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__b311351817514">                //算子信息库定义文件</strong>
│   └── toolchain.cmake

│       └── ai_core
│                 ├── {Soc Version}       //<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__ph66891636172619">昇腾AI处理器</span>类型

<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__p0212344719">ini文件在TBE算子工程下的路径为：<span class="filepath" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__filepath19343173810501">“tbe/op_info_cfg/ai_core/{Soc Version}/xx.ini”</span>，<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i742420464564">{Soc Version}为</em><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__ph1223711129494">昇腾AI处理器</span>的类型。</p>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__p14456515210">ini文件在AI CPU算子工程下的路径为：<span class="filepath" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__zh-cn_topic_0290818047_filepath19343173810501">“cpukernel/op_info_cfg/aicpu_kernel/xx.ini”</span>。</p>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__note155299108178"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__p0992111852013">若进行AI CPU自定义算子ST测试，请不要改变算子工程的目录结构。因为该工具会根据算子信息库定义文件所在算子工程的目录找到算子原型定义文件，并根据算子原型定义文件生成算子测试用例定义文件。</p>
</div></div>

<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__p1547035215113">测试用例定义文件其他配置项及说明。</p>
<ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__ul366814571312"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li166681158135">当TBE算子信息库定义文件（*.ini）中input<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i1666875191320">x</em>.paramType=optional时，生成的算子测试用例中input<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i46680581317">x</em>的"format"为"UNDEFINED"或"RESERVED"，"type"为"UNDEFINED"。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li4668175191313">当TBE算子信息库定义文件（*.ini）中input<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i16668553137">x</em>.paramType=dynamic时，生成的算子测试用例中input<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__i126685515131">x</em>的"name"为“算子名称+编号”，编号根据dynamic_input的个数确定，从0开始依次递增。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li46691754138">当TBE算子信息库定义文件（*.ini）中Tensor的实现format与原始format不同时，用户需手动添加"ori_format"和"ori_shape"字段，将origin_format与origin_shape转成离线模型需要的format与shape。<ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__ul17669159136"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li966912591315">"ori_format"输入为原始算子支持的数据格式，个数必须与format个数保持一致。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187205__li126698541317">"ori_shape"输入为shape根据format和ori_format转换的结果。</li></ul>
</li></ul>

    x_shape_2 = random.randint(1, 64)
    x_shape_3 = random.randint(1, 64)
    if dim == 1:
        shape = [x_shape_0]
    if dim == 2:
        shape = [x_shape_0, x_shape_1]
    if dim == 3:
        shape = [x_shape_0, x_shape_1, x_shape_2]
    if dim == 4:
        shape = [x_shape_0, x_shape_1, x_shape_2, x_shape_3]
    # 根据shape随机生成x1、x2的value
    fuzz_value_x1 = np.random.randint(1, 10, size=shape)
    fuzz_value_x2 = np.random.randint(1, 10, size=shape)

    # 用字典数据结构返回shape值，将生成的shape值返回给input_desc的x1、x2和output_desc的y的shape参数。其中x1、x2、y测试用例定义文件输入、输出的name。
    return {"input_desc": {"x1": {"shape": shape,"value": fuzz_value_x1}, 
                           "x2": {"shape": shape,"value": fuzz_value_x2}},
            "output_desc": {"y": {"shape": shape}}}</pre>
