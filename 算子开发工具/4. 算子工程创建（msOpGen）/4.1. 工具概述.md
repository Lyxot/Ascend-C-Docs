    <h4 class="sectiontitle">工具概述</h4>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__p1615015571462">完成算子分析&amp;原型定义后，可使用msOpGen工具生成自定义算子工程，并进行编译部署，具体流程请参考<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__fig1120319585112">图1</a>。</p>
    <div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__note11346121772920">
     <img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span>
     <div class="notebody">
      <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__p1634614176294">在TBE及AI CPU算子开发场景中，msOpGen工具的使用详情请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_10_0024.html">基于msOpGen工具创建算子工程</a>及<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_10_0087.html">算子编译部署</a>。</p>
     </div>
    </div>
    <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__p1223773717263">具有如下功能：</p>
    <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__zh-cn_topic_0000001776910254_ul16529162618371">
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__zh-cn_topic_0000001776910254_li1352942618371">基于算子原型定义输出算子工程。</li>
     <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189102__zh-cn_topic_0000001776910254_li10787145513208">基于性能仿真环境生成的dump数据文件输出算子仿真流水图文件。</li>
    </ul>
