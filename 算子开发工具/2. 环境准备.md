<h1 class="topictitle1">环境准备</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">环境要求</h4><p>进行算子开发之前，需要安装驱动固件和CANN Toolkit软件包，<span>请参考<span><a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/softwareinst/instg/instg_0008.html?Mode=PmIns&amp;InstallType=local&amp;OS=Debian&amp;Software=cannToolKit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">安装CANN</a></span>中选择“训练&amp;推理&amp;开发调试”场景安装CANN软件包。</span>本节不再给出安装示例。</p>
<div class="note"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><ul><li><span>${git_clone_path}</span>为sample仓的存放路径。</li><li>可参照<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0080.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370267081__zh-cn_topic_0000001850780656_li064693084719">步骤1</a>检查宿主机是否使用--debug选项安装HDK驱动包。</li><li>若要使能<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0061.html">msDebug工具</a>，需通过以下两种方法安装NPU驱动固件（CANN 8.1.RC1之后的版本且驱动为25.0.RC1之后的版本，推荐使用方法一）：<ul><li>方法一：驱动安装时指定<strong>--full</strong>参数，然后再使用root用户执行echo 1 &gt; /proc/debug_switch命令打开调试通道，msDebug工具便可正常使用。<pre class="code_wrap">./Ascend-hdk-&lt;chip_type&gt;-npu-driver_&lt;version&gt;_linux-&lt;arch&gt;.run --full</pre>
</li><li>方法二：驱动安装时指定<strong>--debug</strong>参数，具体安装操作请参见<span><a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/softwareinst/instg/instg_0005.html?Mode=PmIns&amp;InstallType=local&amp;OS=Debian&amp;Software=cannToolKit" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">安装NPU驱动和固件</a></span>。<pre class="code_wrap">./Ascend-hdk-&lt;chip_type&gt;-npu-driver_&lt;version&gt;_linux-&lt;arch&gt;.run --debug</pre>
</li></ul>
</li></ul>
</div></div>

</li></ul>
