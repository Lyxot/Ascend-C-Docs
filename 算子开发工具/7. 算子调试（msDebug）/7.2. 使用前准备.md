<h1 class="topictitle1">使用前准备</h1>
<div id="body8662426"><div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__section226622601514"><h4 class="sectiontitle">环境准备</h4><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__p16325941513"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__ph1668547842">请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0003.html">环境准备</a>，完成相关环境变量的配置。</span></p>
</div>
<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831604757_section1754465113486"><h4 class="sectiontitle">使用约束</h4><ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831604757_ul1174518913158"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831604757_li89674449259">若要使能msDebug工具需要安装驱动，具体安装方法请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0062.html">工具概述</a>。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831604757_li1390175617193">单Device仅支持使用单个msDebug工具进行调试，且不推荐同时运行其他算子程序。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831604757_li153011915101514">当被调试程序调用多个算子时，msDebug工具仅支持对指定的单个算子进行调试。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831604757_li42601624141512">调试算子时，溢出检测功能会关闭。</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__section18727927191814"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__section18727927191814"></a><a name="section18727927191814"></a><h4 class="sectiontitle">导入调试信息</h4><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__p193391435131814">算子调试前，需先启用调试-g -O0编译选项重新编译，使算子二进制带上调试信息，具体方法可参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0074.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187201__zh-cn_topic_0000001795016076_li339451095417">基于样例工程编译算子</a>。</p>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__note52501032201012"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__p1125053261019">在-O0编译选项场景下，算子程序的行为与-O2编译场景会不一致。因此算子内部的竞争问题不建议在-O0编译选项下定位，推荐使用msSanitizer工具的<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0043.html">竞争检测</a>功能进行定位。</p>
</div></div>
<div class="p" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__p5101175818412">通常情况下，算子调试信息会自动被导入msDebug工具。但算子二进制以<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__b10368105934111">.o</strong>文件形式独立存在并部署的情况下（例如<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0075.html">通过Ascend CL单算子调用的场景</a>），需要选择如下方法导入算子调试信息：<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_note164455654312"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_p464415664318">多算子场景时，仅支持导入指定单算子的调试信息，不支持导入多算子的调试信息，且仅支持对指定单算子的.o文件进行调试。</p>
</div></div>
<ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_ul69730613449"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_li1716125685813">方法一：在调试前，配置如下环境变量，指定算子加载路径，导入调试信息。<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_screen69464583582">export LAUNCH_KERNEL_PATH=<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i18508142217202">{</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i450882272018">path_to_kernel}</em>/my_kernel.o  //<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i13596436359">{</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i198681240143720">path_to_kernel}</em>为kernel侧.o文件所在目录</pre>
</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_li116451959204315">方法二：在执行run命令前，执行image add命令，指定算子加载路径，导入调试信息。<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_screen151017113238">(msdebug) image add <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i4168115224911">{path_to_kernel}</em>/my_kernel.o   //<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i108546325492">{</em><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i17854183294917">path_to_kernel}</em>为Kernel侧.o文件所在目录</pre>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_note53203819507"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_ul47163215113"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_li1171732115112">image add仅适用于PyTorch场景的导入方式。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_li1964416566438">若需要程序运行后导入调试信息，还需执行image load命令完成算子调试信息的加载。<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_screen10644105644318">(msdebug) image load -f <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001795841380_i2071631119538">{path_to_kernel}</em>/my_kernel.o -s 0 </pre>
</li></ul>
</div></div>
</li></ul>
</div>

<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__section416872113450"><h4 class="sectiontitle">启动工具</h4><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_p747616771">msDebug工具支持以下两种启动方式：</p>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_note18311153165619"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><div class="p" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_p206085711569">若工具弹出<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_b1631183145618">Cannot read termcap database; using dumb terminal settings.</strong> 的提示信息，可以通过配置export TERMINFO=<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_i1331114316567">xx</em>消除提示，<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_i143111735569">xx</em>为本地TERMINFO路径：<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_screen1731173175613">export TERMINFO=<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_i93123319567">xx</em>    //xx信息可通过infocmp -D命令查询，可以选择符合当前终端配置的路径作为TERMINFO值</pre>
</div>
</div></div>
<ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_ul1881314185716"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_li1474531191717">加载可执行文件<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__i7956171574520">application</em>。<ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_ol774721121015"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_li375122117101">算子编译后可获取NPU侧可执行文件<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__i659125174111">application</em>。<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_note17652194418114"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__p9322353205610">基于<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_ph10444143921214">Ascend C</span>算子的Kernel侧框架执行一键式编译运行，可生成NPU侧可执行文件<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__i733835720416">application</em>，具体操作可参考<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__ph193189572486">Kernel直调</span>。</p>
</div></div>
</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_li76111834115817">输入如下命令，使用msDebug工具加载可执行文件。<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_screen14549124213582">$ <strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_b37936459388">msdebug </strong><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__i111261391429"><strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__b209931015194218">./application</strong></em></pre>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_note1015218181419"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><div class="p" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_p6679319195620">若可执行文件有其他入参，则按照如下形式传入入参：<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_screen122441718133212">msdebug -- <em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__i10681324593">./application --flag1 arg1 --flag2 args2 ...</em></pre>
</div>
</div></div>
</li></ol>
</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_li1881418181072">加载调用算子的Python脚本<ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_ol64011517141318"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_li340117173132">完成了<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_ph18711115104319">PyTorch</span>框架的适配插件开发后，即可实现从<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_ph19387174593715">PyTorch</span>框架调用Ascend C自定义算子，可以通过自定义Python脚本<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_i15312143116203">test_ops_custom.py</em>调用算子。<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_note1989612599215"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__zh-cn_topic_0000001831484761_p1836817415224">通过PyTorch框架进行单算子调用的场景，详细信息可参考<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__ph257119011325"><a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/Pytorch/600/modthirdparty/modparts/thirdpart_0012.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">基于OpPlugin算子适配开发</a></span><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187137__ph85531654132520"></span>。</p>
