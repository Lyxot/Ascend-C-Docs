<h1 class="topictitle1">sanitizerRtMemcpy2d</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>调用aclrtMemcpy2d接口完成矩阵数据内存复制，并向检测工具上报内存复制信息。实际的矩阵数据内存复制行为和参数含义与aclrtMemcpy2d一致。</p>
<div class="note"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p>可参见<span>内存管理</span>章节查看aclrtMemcpy2d的详细说明。</p>
</div></div>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><pre class="screen">aclError sanitizerRtMemcpy2d(void *dst, size_t dpitch, const void *src, size_t spitch, size_t width, size_t height, aclrtMemcpyKind kind, char const *filename, int lineno);</pre>
