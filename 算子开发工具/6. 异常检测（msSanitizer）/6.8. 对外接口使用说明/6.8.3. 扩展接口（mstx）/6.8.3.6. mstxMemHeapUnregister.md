# mstxMemHeapUnregister

### 函数原型
void mstxMemHeapUnregister(mstxDomainHandle_t domain, mstxMemHeapHandle_t heap)

### 功能说明
注销内存池时，与之关联的Regions将一并被注销。

<caption>表1 参数说明</caption>
<thead><tr><th align="left">参数</th><th align="left">输入/输出</th><th align="left">说明</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td valign="top">domain</td><td valign="top">输入</td><td valign="top">domain为内存池所属的域，为globalDomain或<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0144.html">mstxDomainCreateA</a>返回的句柄。<br>数据类型：const char *。</td></tr>
<tr><td valign="top">heap</td><td valign="top">输入</td><td valign="top">heap为需要注销内存池的句柄，为<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0145.html">mstxMemHeapRegister</a>的返回值。<pre>struct mstxMemHeap_st;
typedef struct mstxMemHeap_st mstxMemHeap_t; 
typedef mstxMemHeap_t* mstxMemHeapHandle_t;</pre></td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

### 返回值
无

### 调用示例
```
mstxMemHeapDesc_t heapDesc{};
mstxMemHeapHandle_t memPool = mstxMemHeapRegister(globalDomain, &heapDesc); // 注册内存池
...
mstxMemHeapUnregister(globalDomain, memPool);                        // 注销内存池
```
