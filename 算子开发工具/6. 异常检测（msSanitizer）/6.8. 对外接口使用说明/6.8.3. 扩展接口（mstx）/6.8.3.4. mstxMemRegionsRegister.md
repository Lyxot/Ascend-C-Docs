<h1 class="topictitle1">mstxMemRegionsRegister</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><p><strong>void mstxMemRegionsRegister(mstxDomainHandle_t domain, mstxMemRegionsRegisterBatch_t const *desc)</strong></p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>注册内存池二次分配。用户需保证RegionsRegister的内存位于<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0145.html">mstxMemHeapRegister</a>注册的范围内，否则工具会提示越界读写。</p>
</div>
