<h1 class="topictitle1">检测CANN软件栈的内存</h1>
<div><p>针对用户程序调用CANN软件栈接口可能出现的内存异常操作场景，msSanitizer检测工具提供了Device相关接口和AscendCL相关接口的内存检测能力，方便用户对程序内存异常操作位置的排查和定位。</p>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">内存泄漏检测使用原理</h4><p>当<strong>npu-smi info</strong>命令查询到的设备内存不断增大时，可使用本工具进行内存泄漏定位定界，若AscendCL系列接口泄漏可支持定位到代码行。</p>
<div class="p">如<a href="#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348874__zh-cn_topic_0000001748761501_fig030518812432">图1</a>所示，CANN软件栈内存操作接口包含两个层级，向下使用驱动侧提供的Device侧接口，向上提供了AscendCL系列接口供用户代码调用。<div class="fignone"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348874__zh-cn_topic_0000001748761501_fig030518812432"></a><a name="zh-cn_topic_0000001748761501_fig030518812432"></a><span class="figcap"><b>图1 </b>内存检测</span><br><img class="eddx" src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/figure/zh-cn_image_0000002336189338.png"></div>
</div>
<p>内存泄漏定位可分为以下步骤：</p>
<ol><li>使能Device系列接口进行泄漏检测，判断内存泄漏是否发生在Host侧。若没有，则定界到Device侧的应用出现泄漏；若有，则通过下一个步骤判断AscendCL接口调用是否发生泄漏；</li><li>使能AscendCL系列接口进行泄漏检测，判断用户代码调用AscendCL接口是否存在泄漏。若没有，则定界为非AscendCL接口调用问题；如果出现泄漏，则通过下一步定位到具体代码行；</li><li>使用msSanitizer检测工具中提供的新接口，对头文件重新编译，再用检测工具拉起检测程序，可定位到未释放内存的分配函数所对应的文件名与代码行号。新接口的详细说明请参见<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0048.html">对外接口使用说明</a>。</li></ol>
</div>

<div class="section"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348874__section1587150123812"></a><a name="section1587150123812"></a><h4 class="sectiontitle">导入API头文件和链接动态库</h4><p>本示例以<span><term>Atlas A2 训练系列产品/Atlas 800I A2 推理产品/A200I A2 Box 异构组件</term></span>的内核调用符场景为例，说明导入msSanitizer API头文件“acl.h”和链接相对应的动态库文件的具体操作步骤，其他类型的自定义工程需根据用户实际构建的脚本进行调整。</p>
<ol><li>单击<a href="https://gitee.com/ascend/samples/tree/v1.5-8.2.RC1/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/3_add_kernellaunch/AddKernelInvocationNeo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Link</a>，获取验证代码的样例工程。<p><div class="note"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><div class="p"><span>下载代码样例时，需执行以下命令指定分支版本。</span><pre class="code_wrap"><span>git clone https://gitee.com/ascend/samples.git -b v1.5-8.2.RC1</span></pre>
</div>
</div></div>
</p></li><li>在<span>${git_clone_path}/samples/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/3_add_kernellaunch/AddKernelInvocationNeo目录中，将main.cpp文件引入的“acl/acl.h”头文件替换为msSanitizer工具提供头文件“acl.h”。</span><p><div class="note"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p>在模板库场景下，需将Ascend C模板库<strong>/examples/common/helper.hpp</strong>路径下的<strong>#include &lt;acl/acl.h&gt;</strong>替换为<strong>#include "acl.h"</strong>，具体操作步骤如下。</p>
<ol type="a"><li>执行以下命令，下载<a href="https://gitee.com/ascend/catlass/tree/catlass-v1-stable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Link</a>中的Ascend C模板库。<pre class="screen">git clone https://gitee.com/ascend/catlass.git -b catlass-v1-stable</pre>
</li><li>进入<strong>/examples/common/</strong><strong>helper.hpp</strong>代码目录。<pre class="screen">cd catlass/examples/common/helper.hpp</pre>
</li><li>将<strong>#include &lt;acl/acl.h&gt;</strong>替换为<strong>#include "acl.h"</strong>。</li></ol>
</div></div>
<pre class="screen">#include "data_utils.h"
#ifndef ASCENDC_CPU_DEBUG
<strong class="code_wrap">// #include "acl/acl.h"</strong>
<strong>// acl/acl.h 替换为 acl.h</strong>
<strong>#include "acl.h"</strong>
extern void add_custom_do(uint32_t blockDim, void *stream, uint8_t *x, uint8_t *y, uint8_t *z);
#else</pre>
</p></li><li>在<span>${git_clone_path}/samples/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/3_add_kernellaunch/AddKernelInvocationNeo目录下编辑CMakeLists.txt文件，导入API头文件路径 <span>${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/tools/mssanitizer/include/acl和动态库路径</span><p><div class="p"><span>${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/tools/mssanitizer/lib64/libascend_acl_hook.so。<div class="note"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><ul><li>模板库场景仅适用于<span><term>Atlas A2 训练系列产品/Atlas 800I A2 推理产品/A200I A2 Box 异构组件</term></span>。</li><li>模板库场景时，可通过以下方式增加检测编译选项。<pre class="screen">-I$ENV{ASCEND_HOME_PATH}/tools/mssanitizer/include/acl 
-L$ENV{ASCEND_HOME_PATH}/tools/mssanitizer/lib64 
-lascend_acl_hook </pre>
</li></ul>
</div></div>
<pre class="screen">add_executable(ascendc_kernels_bbit ${CMAKE_CURRENT_SOURCE_DIR}/main.cpp)

target_compile_options(ascendc_kernels_bbit PRIVATE
    $&lt;BUILD_INTERFACE:$&lt;$&lt;STREQUAL:${RUN_MODE},cpu&gt;:-g&gt;&gt;
    -O2 -std=c++17 -D_GLIBCXX_USE_CXX11_ABI=0 -Wall -Werror
)
<strong># 算子可执行文件编译时，引入API头文件路径</strong>
<strong>target_include_directories(ascendc_kernels_bbit PUBLIC</strong>
<strong>    $ENV{ASCEND_HOME_PATH}/tools/mssanitizer/include/acl)</strong>

<strong>target_link_directories(ascendc_kernels_bbit PRIVATE</strong>
<strong>    $ENV{ASCEND_HOME_PATH}/tools/mssanitizer/lib64)</strong>

target_link_libraries(ascendc_kernels_bbit PRIVATE
    $&lt;BUILD_INTERFACE:$&lt;$&lt;OR:$&lt;STREQUAL:${RUN_MODE},npu&gt;,$&lt;STREQUAL:${RUN_MODE},sim&gt;&gt;:host_intf_pub&gt;&gt;

    ascendc_kernels_${RUN_MODE}
    <strong># 将算子可执行文件链接至libascend_acl_hook.so动态库</strong>
    <strong>ascend_acl_hook</strong>
)</pre>
