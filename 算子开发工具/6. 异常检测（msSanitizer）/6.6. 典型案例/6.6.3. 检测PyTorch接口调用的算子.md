<h1 class="topictitle1">检测PyTorch接口调用的算子</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">前提条件</h4><ul><li>单击<a href="https://gitee.com/ascend/samples/tree/v1.5-8.2.RC1/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/1_add_frameworklaunch/AddCustom" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Link</a>获取样例工程，为进行算子检测做准备。<div class="note"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><ul><li>此样例工程仅支持Python3.9，若要在其他Python版本上运行，需要修改<span>${git_clone_path}</span>/samples/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/1_add_frameworklaunch/PytorchInvocation目录下run_op_plugin.sh文件中的Python版本。</li><li>此样例工程不支持<span><term>Atlas A3 训练系列产品</term></span>。</li><li>下载代码样例时，需执行以下命令指定分支版本。<pre class="code_wrap">git clone https://gitee.com/ascend/samples.git -b v1.5-8.2.RC1</pre>
</li></ul>
</div></div>
