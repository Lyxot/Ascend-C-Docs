<h1 class="topictitle1">msSanitizer工具使用"--cce-enable-sanitizer -g"编译算子时出现"InputSection too large"错误</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">现象描述</h4><p>报错ld.lld: error: InputSection too large for range extension thunk。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">原因分析</h4><p>算子链接时输入代码段过大，超过编译器支持的指令跳转范围。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">解决措施</h4><p>通过增加编译选项，启用编译器扩大跳转范围的特性来解决。在<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0040.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189062__li108561313124319">算子代码编译</a>选项"--cce-enable-sanitizer -g"后增加"-Xaicore-start -mcmodel=large -mllvm -cce-aicore-relax -Xaicore-end"。</p>
