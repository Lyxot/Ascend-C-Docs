  <h1 class="topictitle1">Cache热力图</h1>
  <div id="body0000002194490412">
   <p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__p5443161416414">针对用户程序Kernel函数内的L2 Cache访问情况，msProf工具可以记录并通过<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ph1666764164017">MindStudio Insight</span>工具进行可视化呈现Cache热力图，该热力图可显示对应指令信息，以便用户优化L2Cache命中率，从而优化算子程序。</p>
   <div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__note101373360146">
    <img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span>
    <div class="notebody">
     <ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ul2335823161318">
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li18537155110576"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph356311115428">若要使用<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph8468183712510"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph12336192381311">MindStudio Insight</span></span>进行查看时，需要单独安装<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph246813719254"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph183858187410">MindStudio Insight</span></span>软件包，具体下载链接请参见</span><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph3468123762510"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_zh-cn_topic_0000002336348794_ph12860149161717">“<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/mindstudio/81RC1/GUI_baseddevelopmenttool/msascendinsightug/Insight_userguide_0005.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">安装与卸载</a>”</span></span>。</li>
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li19184134662710">将visualize_data.bin文件导入<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370267061_zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_ph12336192381311">MindStudio Insight</span>的具体操作请参考<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002370267061_zh-cn_topic_0000002370187101_ph102291626105020">导入性能数据</span>。</li>
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li129378581081"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ph452145518400">MindStudio Insight</span>具体操作和详细字段解释请参考<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ph9579171018131"><a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/mindstudio/81RC1/GUI_baseddevelopmenttool/msascendinsightug/Insight_userguide_0068.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">源码（Source）</a></span>。</li>
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li12224101543012"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ph4773113311302">添加-g编译选项会在生成的二进制文件中附带调试信息，建议限制带有调试信息的用户程序的访问权限，确保只有授权人员可以访问该二进制文件。</span></li>
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li15242169244"><span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ph8945618527">若不使用llvm-symbolizer组件提供的相关功能，输入msProf的程序编译时不包含-g即可，msProf工具则不会调用llvm-symbolizer组件的相关功能。</span></li>
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li15559135863712">Cache热力图功能不适用于<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__ph1824584213819"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__zh-cn_topic_0000002336176798_term4363218112215">
         Atlas 推理系列产品
        </term></span>。</li>
      <li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370187173__li769101615473">MC2算子和LCCL算子均不支持生成<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0158.html">Cache热力图</a>。</li>
     </ul>
