<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__section15215201620113"><a name="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__section15215201620113"></a><a name="section15215201620113"></a><h4 class="sectiontitle">msprof op simulator配置</h4><div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__note05583735218"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__p118181310103110">msProf工具的仿真功能仅支持单卡场景，无法仿真多卡环境，代码中也只能设置0卡。若修改可见卡号，则会导致仿真失败。</p>
</div></div>
<ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__ul5994333193415"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__zh-cn_topic_0000001752643572_li18287547193215">msProf工具使用--config模式进行算子仿真调优之前，需执行如下命令配置环境变量。<pre class=" code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__zh-cn_topic_0000001752643572_screen1774042011344">export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__ph10562197165916">${INSTALL_DIR}</span>/tools/simulator/Ascend<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__zh-cn_topic_0000001752643572_i2583153823617">xxxyy</em>/lib:$LD_LIBRARY_PATH </pre>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__zh-cn_topic_0000001752643572_p1829165417361">请根据CANN软件包实际安装路径和<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348794__zh-cn_topic_0000001752643572_ph19994533233">昇腾AI处理器</span>的型号对以上环境变量进行修改。</p>
</li></ul>
