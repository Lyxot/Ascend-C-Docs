  <h1 class="topictitle1">通算流水图</h1>
  <div>
   <p>通过<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_00851.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370266965__section0449171943115">msprof op</a>对通算融合算子进行调优后，生成的trace.json和visualize_data.bin文件可通过<span>MindStudio Insight</span>进行可视化呈现，能够直观看到通算运行情况、指令耗时等信息，协助开发者识别通算瓶颈。当前仅支持MC2和LCCL类型的通算融合算子。</p>
   <div class="note">
    <img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span>
    <div class="notebody">
     <ul>
      <li><span>若要使用<span><span>MindStudio Insight</span></span>进行查看时，需要单独安装<span><span>MindStudio Insight</span></span>软件包，具体下载链接请参见</span><span><span>“<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/mindstudio/81RC1/GUI_baseddevelopmenttool/msascendinsightug/Insight_userguide_0005.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">安装与卸载</a>”</span></span>。</li>
      <li>将visualize_data.bin文件导入<span>MindStudio Insight</span>的具体操作请参考<span>导入性能数据</span>。</li>
      <li><span>MindStudio Insight</span>具体操作和详细字段解释请参考<span>《<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/mindstudio/81RC1/GUI_baseddevelopmenttool/msascendinsightug/Insight_userguide_0002.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MindStudio Insight工具用户指南</a>》</span>的<span>“系统调优 &gt; <a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/mindstudio/81RC1/GUI_baseddevelopmenttool/msascendinsightug/Insight_userguide_0034.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">时间线（Timeline）</a>”</span>章节。</li>
      <li><span>添加-g编译选项会在生成的二进制文件中附带调试信息，建议限制带有调试信息的用户程序的访问权限，确保只有授权人员可以访问该二进制文件。</span></li>
     </ul>
