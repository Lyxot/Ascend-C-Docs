<h1 class="topictitle1">采集Ascend C算子的性能数据</h1>
<div id="body0000001969027856"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__p101171372537">展示如何使用msProf工具来上板调优一个vector算子，该vector算子可实现两个向量相加并输出结果的功能。</p>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__note145572611719"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__p791961014710"><a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0082.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348770__zh-cn_topic_0000001740005733_li12291456391">Kernel直调</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0082.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348770__zh-cn_topic_0000001740005733_li112151547153912">单算子API调用</a>和<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0082.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348770__zh-cn_topic_0000001740005733_li146811123103813">PyTorch框架</a>三种算子调用场景下进行性能采集的操作步骤基本一致，本示例以Kernel直调场景为例进行介绍。</p>
</div></div>
<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__section174881844185411"><h4 class="sectiontitle">前提条件</h4><ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ul68657522549"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li138656529540">单击<a href="https://gitee.com/ascend/samples/tree/v1.5-8.2.RC1/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/3_add_kernellaunch/AddKernelInvocationNeo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Link</a>获取样例工程，为进行算子上板和仿真调优做准备。<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__note32301987362"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ul159501620105114"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li44319188466">此样例工程不支持<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000001740005657_ph38331631115919"><term id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002128255878_term1253731311225">Atlas A3 训练系列产品</term></span>。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li73993535118">下载代码样例时，需执行以下命令指定分支版本。<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002370267101_screen798617901320">git clone https://gitee.com/ascend/samples.git -b v1.5-8.2.RC1</pre>
</li></ul>
</div></div>
</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li3515239135">参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0083.html">使用前准备</a>完成相关环境变量配置。</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="section" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__section19971201625514"><h4 class="sectiontitle">操作步骤</h4><ol id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ol193582525516"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li4281103012376"><span>基于样例工程的说明，并参考<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ph193189572486">Kernel直调</span>，完成算子编译前的准备工作。</span></li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li146241945466"><span>构建单算子可执行文件。</span><p><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__p1699845202610">以Add算子为例，在样例工程的<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ph74106543186">${git_clone_path}</span>/samples/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/3_add_kernellaunch/AddKernelInvocationNeo目录下，执行以下命令，构建可执行文件。</p>
<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__screen04934117556">bash run.sh -r npu -v <strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__b165034195512"><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__i450174175520">&lt;soc_version&gt;</em></strong>  # 运行在昇腾设备上的算子
bash run.sh -r sim -v <strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__b1150641205510"><em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__i550134175517">&lt;soc_version&gt;</em></strong>  # 运行在仿真器上的算子</pre>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__p8501441155516">一键式编译运行脚本完成后，在工程目录下生成NPU侧可执行文件ascendc_kernels_bbit。</p>
<div class="note" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__note141771118688"><img src="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/public_sys-resources/note_3.0-zh-cn.png"><span class="notetitle"> </span><div class="notebody"><ul id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ul1553919272419"><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li11832172771017">本示例中可执行文件的名称（ascendc_kernels_bbit）仅为示例，具体以当前工程中用户实际编译的脚本为准。</li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li1091618548489">在安装<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002015877373_ph26061361142">昇腾AI处理器</span>的服务器执行<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002015877373_b116061067419">npu-smi info</strong>命令进行查询，获取<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002015877373_b15606662046">Chip Name</strong>信息。实际配置值为AscendChip Name，例如<strong id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002015877373_b186061865415">Chip Name</strong>取值为<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002015877373_i1560656248">xxxyy</em>，实际配置值为Ascend<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__zh-cn_topic_0000002015877373_i9606666418">xxxyy</em>。</li></ul>
</div></div>
</p></li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li17219196784"><span>导入环境变量。</span><p><pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__screen18891132811817">export LD_LIBRARY_PATH=<span id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__ph8247131151519">${git_clone_path}</span>/samples/operator/ascendc/0_introduction/3_add_kernellaunch/AddKernelInvocationNeo/out/lib/:$LD_LIBRARY_PATH</pre>
</p></li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li17501541115519"><span>采集算子性能数据。</span><p><p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__p75010414552">对于运行在昇腾设备上的算子，使用如下命令完成<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_00851.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370266965__section0449171943115">msprof op</a>性能数据和精细化调优数据的采集。</p>
<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__screen85034110555">msprof op ascendc_kernels_bbit</pre>
<p id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__p2509419557">对于运行在仿真器上的算子，使用如下命令完成<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_00851.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002370266965__section8684154219309">msprof op simulator</a>性能数据、流水图和热点图数据的采集。</p>
<pre class="code_wrap" id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__screen35044110558">msprof op simulator --soc-version=Ascend<em id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__i1440121713511">xxxyy</em> ascendc_kernels_bbit  // xxxyy为用户实际使用的具体芯片类型</pre>
</p></li><li id="ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336188986__li16501741135515"><span>查看算子性能数据，具体请参见<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_00851.html">工具使用</a>章节。</span></li></ol>
