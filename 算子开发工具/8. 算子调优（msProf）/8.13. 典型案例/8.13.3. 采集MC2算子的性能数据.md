<h1 class="topictitle1">采集MC2算子的性能数据</h1>
<div><p>展示如何使用msProf工具来上板调优一个MC2算子，并生成通算流水图。</p>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">前提条件</h4><ul><li>完成MC2算子的开发。</li><li>参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0083.html">使用前准备</a>完成相关环境变量配置。</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">操作步骤</h4><p>本示例以<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0082.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336348770__zh-cn_topic_0000001740005733_li112151547153912">Ascend CL单算子调用</a>为例，其他调用场景请参见<span><span>《<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/opdevg/Ascendcopdevg/atlas_ascendc_10_0001.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Ascend C算子开发指南</a>》</span></span>。</p>
<ol><li>请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0024.html">算子编译部署</a>，完成算子的编译部署。<p><ol type="a"><li>在算子编译文件op_kernel目录下的CMakeLists.txt中引入以下编译选项，使能MC2算子的AIC打点和代码行映射功能。<pre class="screen">add_ops_compile_options(<strong>ALL OPTIONS -DASCENDC_TIME_STAMP_ON, -g</strong>)</pre>
</li><li>进入自定义算子工程目录下编译部署算子。<pre class="code_wrap">./build_out/<strong>custom_opp_</strong>&lt;target_os&gt;_&lt;target_architecture&gt;<strong>.run</strong></pre>
</li></ol>
</p></li><li>使用msProf采集MC2算子的性能数据。<p><pre class="code_wrap"><strong>msprof</strong> <strong>op </strong><strong><em>--output</em></strong>=$HOME/projects/output $HOME/projects/MyApp blockdim 1 //<strong><em> --output</em></strong>为可选参数,$HOME/projects/MyApp为使用的app,blockdim 1为用户app的可选参数 </pre>
</p></li><li>界面生成以下目录结构和性能数据文件，具体请参见<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0131.html">msprof op</a>章节。</li><li>将trace.json或visualize_data.bin文件导入MindStudio Insight工具进行可视化呈现，具体请参见<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0086.html">计算内存热力图</a>、<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0139.html">通算流水图</a>和<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0119.html">Roofline瓶颈分析图</a>。</li></ol>
</div>
