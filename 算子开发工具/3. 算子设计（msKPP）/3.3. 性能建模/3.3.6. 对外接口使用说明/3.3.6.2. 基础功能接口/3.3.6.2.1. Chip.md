<h1 class="topictitle1">Chip</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>处理器抽象，在with语句中实例化并用来明确针对某一<span>昇腾AI处理器</span>类型进行建模。</p>
</div>

<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">成员</h4>
<div class="tablenoborder"><table cellpadding="4" cellspacing="0" summary="" frame="border" border="1" rules="all"><thead align="left"><tr><th align="left" class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="23.34%"><p>成员名称</p>
</th>
<th align="left" class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="76.66%"><p>描述</p>
</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr><td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="23.34%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.1 "><p>chip.enable_trace()</p>
</td>
<td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="76.66%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.2 "><p>使能算子模拟流水图的功能，生成流水图文件trace.json。</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr><td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="23.34%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.1 "><p>chip.enable_metrics()</p>
</td>
<td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="76.66%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.2 "><p>使能单指令及分PIPE的流水信息，生成指令统计（Instruction_statistic.csv）、搬运流水统计（Pipe_statistic.csv）文件和指令占比饼图（instruction_cycle_consumption.html）。</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr><td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="23.34%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.1 "><p>chip.set_cache_hit_ratio(config)</p>
</td>
<td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="76.66%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.2 "><p>用于使能手动调整L2Cache命中率，其中config = {"cache_hit_ratio": 0.6}，具体介绍请参见<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0009.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189066__zh-cn_topic_0000001692046854_li173978547417">支持cache命中率建模</a>。</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr><td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="23.34%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.1 "><p>chip.set_prof_summary_path("xxx/PipeUtilization.csv")</p>
</td>
<td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="76.66%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.2 "><p>其中PipeUtilization.csv为msprof的结果示例，用于使能PIPE信息的理论值与msprof实测值比对。具体介绍请参见<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/devaids/optool/atlasopdev_16_0009.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336189066__zh-cn_topic_0000001692046854_li47891488449">支持PIPE信息的理论值与msprof实测值比对</a>。</p>
</td>
</tr>
<tr><td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="23.34%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.1 "><p>chip.disable_instr_log()</p>
</td>
<td class="cellrowborder" valign="top" width="76.66%" headers="mcps1.3.4.2.1.3.1.2 "><p>使能后，抑制指令任务添加和调度结束后的日志打印。</p>
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
