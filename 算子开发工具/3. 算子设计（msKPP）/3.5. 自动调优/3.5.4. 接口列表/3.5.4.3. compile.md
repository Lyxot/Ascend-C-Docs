<h1 class="topictitle1">compile</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>编译Kernel下发代码，返回一个可执行的Kernel对象。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><pre class="screen">kernel = compile(build_script, gen_file)</pre>

<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">返回值说明</h4><p>可运行的Kernel对象，类型：CompiledKernel，支持如下方式调用kernel：kernel[blockdim](arg1, arg2, ..., timeout=-1, device_id=0, repeat=1)，其中arg1、arg2、...是Kernel的入参。</p>
<p>调用示例</p>
<pre class="screen">kernel = compile(build_script, gen_file)
kernel[blockdim](arg1, arg2, ..., device_id=0)</pre>
