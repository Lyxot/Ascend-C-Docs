<h1 class="topictitle1">compile_executable</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">功能说明</h4><p>编译代码，返回一个可执行的executable对象。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">函数原型</h4><pre class="screen">executable = compile_executable(build_script, src_file)</pre>
