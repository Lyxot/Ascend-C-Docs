<h1 class="topictitle1">环境准备</h1>
<div><p>进行<span>Ascend C</span>算子开发之前，需要安装<strong>驱动固件</strong>和<strong>CANN软件包</strong>，完成环境准备。</p>
<p><strong>驱动固件</strong>（仅昇腾设备需要）的安装步骤可参见<span>《<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/softwareinst/instg/instg_quick.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CANN 软件安装指南</a>》</span>的“安装NPU驱动固件”章节，本节不再给出安装示例。</p>
<p>本节以训练&amp;推理&amp;开发调试业务场景、操作系统架构X86_64、root用户操作为例，介绍<strong>CANN软件包</strong>安装的步骤，其他场景的安装步骤类似，详细的可参见<span>《<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/document/detail/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/softwareinst/instg/instg_quick.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">CANN 软件安装指南</a>》</span>。</p>
