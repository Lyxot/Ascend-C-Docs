<h1 class="topictitle1">kernel侧获取Tiling信息不正确</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">现象描述</h4><p>通过算子在kernel侧实现代码中添加PRINTF打印发现kernel侧获取的Tiling信息不正确。</p>
<p>比如下文样例，增加的打印代码如下：</p>
<div class="codecoloring" codetype="Cpp"><div class="highlight"><table class="highlighttable"><tr><td class="linenos"><div class="linenodiv"><pre><span class="normal">1</span></pre></div></td><td class="code"><div><pre><span class="n">PRINTF</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">&quot;tiling_data.totalLength: %d tiling_data.tileNum: %d.</span><span class="se">\n</span><span class="s">&quot;</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">tiling_data</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="n">totalLength</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">tiling_data</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="n">tileNum</span><span class="p">);</span>
</pre></div></td></tr></table></div>
</div>
<p>打印的Tiling数据如下，全为0：</p>
<div class="codecoloring" codetype="ColdFusion"><div class="highlight"><table class="highlighttable"><tr><td class="linenos"><div class="linenodiv"><pre><span class="normal">1</span></pre></div></td><td class="code"><div><pre><span class="nv">tiling_data.totalLength</span><span class="p">:</span> <span class="m">0</span> <span class="nv">tiling_data.tileNum</span><span class="p">:</span> <span class="m">0</span><span class="p">.</span>
</pre></div></td></tr></table></div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">问题根因</h4><p>kernel侧获取Tiling信息不正确的原因一般有以下两种：</p>
<ul><li>host侧计算Tiling的逻辑不正确</li><li>kernel侧核函数的参数未按照正确顺序填写</li></ul>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">处理步骤</h4><ol><li>参考如下示例，打印TilingData的数据，确认host侧序列化保存的TilingData是否正确。如果此时打印值有误，说明Tiling的计算逻辑可能不正确，需要进一步检查host侧Tiling实现代码，排查计算逻辑是否有误。<p><div class="codecoloring" codetype="Cpp"><div class="highlight"><table class="highlighttable"><tr><td class="linenos"><div class="linenodiv"><pre><span class="normal">1</span></pre></div></td><td class="code"><div><pre><span class="n">std</span><span class="o">::</span><span class="n">cout</span><span class="o">&lt;&lt;*</span><span class="k">reinterpret_cast</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="kt">uint32_t</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">*&gt;</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">context</span><span class="o">-&gt;</span><span class="n">GetRawTilingData</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="o">-&gt;</span><span class="n">GetData</span><span class="p">())</span><span class="o">&lt;&lt;</span><span class="n">std</span><span class="o">::</span><span class="n">endl</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="c1">//按照实际数据类型打印TilingData第一个参数值，如需确认其他值，取值指针向后偏移即可</span>
</pre></div></td></tr></table></div>
</div>
</p></li><li>如果上一步骤中打印的TilingData正确，需要排查kernel侧核函数的参数是否按照正确顺序填写。<p><p>使用msOpGen工具创建算子工程，并基于工程进行kernel侧算子开发时，核函数的定义模板已通过msOpGen工具自动生成，样例如下所示<strong>。</strong>参数按照 “输入、输出、workspace、tiling”的顺序排布。请检查是否调整过参数顺序导致和正确顺序不一致。</p>
