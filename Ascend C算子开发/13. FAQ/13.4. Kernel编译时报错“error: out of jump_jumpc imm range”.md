<h1 class="topictitle1">Kernel编译时报错“error: out of jump/jumpc imm range”</h1>
<div><div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">现象描述</h4><p>使用工程化算子开发方式，基于自定义算子工程进行算子开发。编译算子时失败，报如下错误：</p>
<div class="codecoloring" codetype="ColdFusion"><div class="highlight"><table class="highlighttable"><tr><td class="linenos"><div class="linenodiv"><pre><span class="normal">1</span></pre></div></td><td class="code"><div><pre><span class="p">[</span><span class="nv">ERROR</span><span class="p">]</span> <span class="p">[</span><span class="nv">ascendxxxx</span><span class="p">]</span> <span class="nv">PowerCustom_88a695f03edfbc0af76b9eaae9e4556c</span> <span class="nv">error</span><span class="p">:</span> <span class="nv">out</span> <span class="nv">of</span> <span class="nv">jump</span><span class="o">/</span><span class="nv">jumpc</span> <span class="nv">imm</span> <span class="nv">range</span>
</pre></div></td></tr></table></div>
</div>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">问题根因</h4><p>该编译错误的原因是算子kernel代码过大，导致在编译时跳转指令跳转的偏移值超过了限定的大小(int16_t的数据范围)，可通过添加编译选项“-mllvm -cce-aicore-jump-expand=true”通过间接跳转的方式来避免该问题，让编译器能够正常编译。</p>
</div>
<div class="section"><h4 class="sectiontitle">处理步骤</h4><ol><li>在kernel侧的CMakeLists中通过add_ops_compile_options针对报错算子添加编译选项“-mllvm -cce-aicore-jump-expand=true”，示例如下：<p><pre class="screen">add_ops_compile_options(PowerCustom OPTIONS -mllvm -cce-aicore-jump-expand=true)</pre>
<p>add_ops_compile_options的具体使用方法请参考<a href="https://www.hiascend.com/doc_center/source/zh/CANNCommunityEdition/82RC1/opdevg/Ascendcopdevg/atlas_ascendc_10_0068.html#ZH-CN_TOPIC_0000002336336606__section7321165972311">支持自定义编译选项</a>。</p>
</p></li><li>重新编译该算子。正常编译无报错。</li></ol>
</div>
